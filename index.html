What we can actually hand out

Six families. For campus sampling, Ultra and Juice do the heavy lifting: zero sugar for gym crowds, flavour for everyone else.

01

Original Green

The one everyone pictures. Full sugar, classic bite, the reference point for the whole category.

Hero SKU

Three activations for this semester

Each one is cheap to run, generates content, and gives the mentor a number to report upward.

01

Fest Fuel Station

A branded counter at the college cultural fest. Free chilled cans against a QR sign-up, claw decals on the barricades, and a photo wall that puts the logo in every uploaded picture. One volunteer counts cans, one collects sign-ups.

  • Sampling
  • Lead capture
  • High footfall
02

Midnight Grind LAN

An overnight campus gaming tournament co-hosted with the esports club. Monster on every desk, a claw-marked prize, and a clipped highlight reel posted the next morning while people are still talking about it.

  • Esports
  • Content
  • Repeatable
03

Campus Crew

Ten students who each post one story a week in exchange for a monthly case. Cheap, always on, and it turns a one-day event into a semester of visibility. Track it with a single shared hashtag so reporting stays honest.

  • Always-on
  • Low cost
  • Measurable
