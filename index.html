Three steps, no paperwork loop

You fill one form. We handle permissions with your college committee, logistics and the crew.

01

Send the request

Name, phone, venue address and your event date. Thirty seconds. Every submission lands with our team instantly, no inbox black hole.

02

We call you back

Usually within two working days. We confirm the date, check the parking and power situation at your venue, and lock what the truck brings.

03

Truck shows up

Crew arrives ahead of your start time, sets up, samples, and clears out clean. You get the photos and the footfall numbers afterwards.
